Glitches & Guides

1. Matte + Pearl Glitch ▼
Effect: Apply Pearlescent over Matte paint.

1. Go to LSC > Respray > Primary > Matte.
2. Buy any Matte color.
3. Go to Metallic, scroll through list for 3-5 seconds (don't buy).
4. Back out, go to Plates (or Wheels), change something.
5. Go back to Respray > Primary > Pearlescent.
6. You can now select a pearl!
2. Patriot Tire Smoke / True Black ▼
Effect: Unlocks rare Patriot Smoke & "Super Black" paint.

1. Use this tool's "Tools" tab > "Copy #000000AA".
2. Go to Social Club Edit Crew page.
3. Use the Injector script from the Tools tab.
4. Save Crew & Restart Game.
5. In LSC, set Crew Color (Paint) & Crew Smoke (Tires).
3. Colored Chrome / "4D" Paint ▼
Effect: Shiny Chrome with a color tint.

1. Go to LSC > Respray > Primary > Chrome.
2. Apply Chrome.
3. Apply Crew Color immediately after.
Note: This preserves the chrome reflection under the color.
4. Secret Gold Paint ▼
Method 1 (Pure):
Metals > Pure Gold > Apply Crew Color (Yellow/Orange tint).

Method 2 (LSC):
Metallic > Bronze > Pearlescent > Race Yellow.
5. Chameleon + Pearl (Next Gen) ▼
Effect: 3-tone paint job.

1. Apply any Chameleon paint.
2. Do the "Matte/Metallic Scroll" glitch (Step 3 in Guide #1).
3. Change Plate.
4. Go to Pearlescent and apply any pearl over the Chameleon.
6. Chameleon + Crew Color ▼
Effect: Unique color-shifting crew paint.

1. Go to LSC.
2. Respray > Primary > Chameleon.
3. Buy any Chameleon color.
4. Respray > Secondary > Chameleon.
5. Buy same Chameleon color as primary.
6. Respray > Primary > Crew.
7. Apply Crew color.
8. Leave LSC.

Notes: Works best with darker crew colors.

Features & Usage

3D Visualizer 🏎️

High-fidelity 3D preview featuring the Annis Elegy RH8 with real-time material simulation (Gloss, Matte, Metal, Chrome).

RAGE Shader Fidelity: Now utilizing a Separated Lobe pearlescent model. This advanced shader produces vibrant "color-shift" highlights from all viewing angles while maintaining perfectly neutral, realistic environment reflections.

Performance & Stability: The engine targets a locked **60 FPS** with dynamic sampling. Real-time planar reflections are optimized to 256x256 to provide the authentic "blurred" grounding seen in GTA V's garage interiors.
Advanced Mixer 🎛️ ▼
Fine-tune your colors using RGB, HSL, and Alpha (Transparency) sliders.

How to use: Adjust the sliders for precise control. Use the Alpha Chips (AA, 00, EE, etc.) to quickly apply common transparency presets used for special crew color effects.
Lighting Scenarios ☀️ ▼
Toggle between different environments to see how your color reacts to various lighting conditions in Los Santos.

Garage: A high-fidelity showroom environment featuring an overhead **RectAreaLight** for soft, diffused highlights. Utilizes high-performance planar reflections for a realistic, grounded floor.

Daylight: Calibrated for High Noon in Los Santos, featuring strong vertical highlights and high-contrast shadowing.

Night / Street: Evaluates how colors behave under artificial yellow-sodium streetlights and soft moonlit conditions.

None: A strictly clinical, high-key studio environment with neutral, uniform illumination designed for pure color analysis.
Magic Correct (Engine Simulation) ✨ ▼
This tool utilizes the Tjakal Algorithm (1.58 Power Law) to bridge the gap between Social Club's hex inputs and the RAGE engine's in-game rendering.

Magic Correct: Automatically reverse-engineers the necessary hex code to compensate for engine washout, allowing you to hit your "Dream Color" exactly as intended.

Engine Simulation: When "Preview True In-Game Result" is active, the visualizer applies the 1.58 gamma lift to show you the precise color you will see on the streets of Los Santos.
Image Color Picker 📷 ▼
Extract colors directly from your reference photos or screenshots with pinpoint accuracy.

How to use: Click the Camera Icon in the Mixer. Upload an image to open the Image Color Picker. Use the Zoom (+/-) buttons to get closer to small details, then click anywhere on the image to select the color.
OEM Library & Search 📚 ▼
Searchable access to 1000+ real-world paint codes curated from manufacturers like Ferrari, Porsche, and BMW.

How to use: Navigate to the Library tab. Use the search bar to find specific colors or use the Filter Chips to browse by manufacturer. Click any swatch to load it into the editor.
Build Manager ⭐ ▼
Save and manage your favorite color combinations with full build details.

How to use: Click SAVE ⭐ in the editor to store your current color. You can add details like Pearlescent, Secondary, and Wheel colors. Access your collection in the Saved tab.

Pro Tips 💡

  • True Black: Use #00000000 or #000000AA for the darkest possible black in GTA.
  • Fluorescent Effect: Use high-saturation colors with AA or FF alpha for a vibrant "glow" effect under certain lights.
  • Pearlescent Stacking: The 3D visualizer simulates how a pearl coat (e.g., Ultra Blue) interacts with your base color. Darker bases make pearls pop more!
  • Fullscreen Comparison: Use the Full Screen mode in the Tools tab to compare your color directly against your TV or monitor to ensure a perfect match.

How To Apply Colors

  1. Create or Select a color in this tool.
  2. Tap COPY HEX (e.g., #FF0000AA).
  3. Go to the Rockstar Social Club "Edit Crew" page.
  4. Desktop: Right-click the color box > "Inspect" > Paste hex into the value field.
  5. Mobile: Copy the "Injector Script" from the Tools tab, create a bookmark, and run it on the Social Club page.
  6. Click "Save Crew" on Social Club.
  7. Restart GTA Online (or swap characters) to refresh crew data.
  8. Go to Los Santos Customs > Respray > Crew Color.
